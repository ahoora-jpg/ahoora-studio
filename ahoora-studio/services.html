Common questions

Before you ask

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You'll be able to update text and images yourself once the site is handed over, with a short walkthrough.

What do you need from me to start?

Your logo (or a rough idea of one), any existing photos, and a short description of what the business needs the site to do.

Does the price include hosting and the domain?

Domain and hosting are billed separately at cost (usually R100–R250/month) — I'll set it up and explain exactly what you're paying for.

What if I need changes after launch?

Small tweaks are covered for 2 weeks after launch. Anything ongoing fits under the care plan above.

Not sure which plan fits?

Tell me about the business and I'll suggest the right scope — no pressure, no obligation.
