The problem

Most SaaS landing pages default to a big gradient headline and a stock illustration of a dashboard — nothing that actually demonstrates what the product does or why it's more reliable than the alternative.

The approach

An interactive 3D node network in the hero — built with real WebGL, not a static image — where connected, glowing nodes literally represent the workflows the product runs. It's decoration that's actually the product's core metaphor, paired with copy that leads with the failure case ("2am in production") instead of generic feature bullets.
